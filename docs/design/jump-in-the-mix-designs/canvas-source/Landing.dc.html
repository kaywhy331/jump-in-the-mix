A draft is a starting point, not a message you have to send. Review and edit before you send through your usual text or email app. Follow-up planning lives here; the conversation stays with you.

Jump in the Mix is a client follow-up web app for independent professionals. It organizes contacts into reusable follow-up plans called Mixes. Each Beat is a scheduled message or call step. You review and send messages yourself, and the app does not read or interpret replies.
