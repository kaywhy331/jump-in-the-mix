## Candidate set

Scores are ordinal editorial judgments, not measured probabilities or percentages. Each vector uses eight 1–5 ratings in this order: source/message match, awareness fit, clarity, specificity, reader relevance, differentiation, credibility, continuity with the existing subhead. A 5 means strong editorial fit; 3 means usable with a notable limitation; 1 means poor fit. Totals have equal weights and only aid shortlisting. Apparent one-point differences are not evidence of superiority. Re-score message match when actual entry campaigns exist.

## Comparison execution

Use the [session worksheet](HOMEPAGE_RESEARCH_SESSION_TEMPLATE_2026-09-10.md) and [research protocol](HOMEPAGE_RESEARCH_PROTOCOL_2026-09-10.md). Recruit the already-planned five real-estate participants and five consultants; no contact has been made. Record the route/build ID and current headline before the unprompted journey. Show alternate cards only afterward, keeping typography, category, subhead, scene and CTA identical.

Rotate card order across participants: H01/H02/H23, H02/H23/H01, H23/H01/H02, then repeat. Record order and prior exposure; the current headline has a familiarity advantage. Ask each participant what the product does, what they expect the demo to do, and what happens after joining. Capture their wording before asking preference. Do not reveal the hypotheses or score vectors.

Primary evidence is correct comprehension per candidate, reported as raw counts and observations within each profession. Guardrails: no mistaken real sending/scheduling or immediate account-access expectation. Any such misconception requires revision and retesting; preference cannot override it. Apply the roadmap's four-of-five usability criteria to the full initial journey, not to a statistically unsupported candidate ranking. Ten repeated-exposure sessions cannot establish conversion lift. If no clear comprehension advantage appears, retain current copy and record uncertainty. Any selection needs a dated owner decision tied to session IDs.
