# WF-03 — Copy change

**Trigger:** any customer-facing words. That includes:
- labels, help text, errors, empty states and confirmations;
- email;
- ready-made Mix and Beat message wording;
- the homepage, profession pages, onboarding and FAQ;
- any marketing.

**Inputs:** the intake plan (WF-01); the current product behavior; JITM-02, JITM-04 and JITM-05.

## Steps

1. **Name the register, and the reader for persuasive copy.** Every string has one register (JITM-04 §2.3):
   - interface text;
   - the owner's personal wording in drafts and messages;
   - marketing.

   For persuasive copy, also name the reader and the ICP hypothesis (JITM-02 §2, JITM-04 §2.25). Interface text needs only its register and the truth rules (JITM-04 §2.24).
2. **Check the vocabulary** against the glossary (JITM-05 §2). Use current terms, and never a retired term or an internal model name.
3. **Build a claims ledger for persuasive copy.** List each claim with its proof and status (JITM-04 §2.26). Every consequential claim is removed, or proven in the ledger and approved by Kevin with a date (JITM-04 §2.28). Public copy ships only with Kevin's approval (JITM-04 §2.31).
   - No invented testimonials, statistics or research.
   - Pricing: state only Kevin's plans and prices (JITM-01 §2.9), and say that paid plans haven't started until charging starts. Never promise a start date, a trial, a discount or what happens to beta accounts. The owner-reviewed facts "free account, no payment card" stay (JITM-02 §2.16, §2.18).
   - No promise of automatic sending or confirmed delivery.
4. **Draft in the register's voice** (JITM-04 §2). Interface text is plain and short. Personal wording sounds like the owner, not like marketing.
5. **Verify every factual statement against current behavior,** in the code or the running app, never against an older document.
6. **Clean-room check:** no third-party framework, method, course or author names (JITM-04 §2.34).
7. **Check it in context.** If the text sits in the UI, capture screenshots in both themes (WF-02, step 5) and check for overflow at narrow widths and at 200% text.

## Evidence to produce

- Before and after strings, with file locations.
- The claims ledger, for persuasive copy.
- Kevin's dated approval for consequential claims and public copy.
- Screenshots when the text is in the UI.
- A NOT RUN list.

## Done when

- Every claim is proven or removed, and every consequential claim carries Kevin's dated approval (JITM-04 §2.28).
- Public copy has Kevin's approval (JITM-04 §2.31).
- The clean-room check passes (JITM-04 §2.34).
- The vocabulary matches JITM-05.
- The text reads correctly in place.

## Stop and ask Kevin when

- New claims touch pricing, outcomes, testimonials, legal or privacy promises.
- A consequential claim or public copy is ready to publish; both need his dated approval (JITM-04 §2.28, §2.31).
- The brand voice or the vocabulary would change.
