## Privacy notice draft

Jump in the Mix helps you organize contacts and decide when to follow up. **[Operator name]** operates the service. Contact **[monitored support email]** about your account, privacy or information someone else has entered about you. Effective **[date]**.

**Information you provide.** We store the account details you submit and the contacts, notes, dates, mixes, follow-up history and preferences you save. Joining the waitlist records your email and confirmation/access status without creating a customer account. Invitations record the sender, recipient and grant/delivery history. Support conversations contain what you choose to send us. Please include only information you are entitled to use and avoid adding unnecessary sensitive information.

**How the service uses information.** We use these records to operate your account, prepare your follow-up list, provide the connections and notifications you enable, deliver requested account/invitation/support emails, answer support requests, protect access and investigate delivery failures or abuse. Operational records support sending limits, recipient opt-outs, recovery and reliability. An invitation or a message handoff is not proof that a recipient read or replied to it.

**Sending and providers.** By default, you review a follow-up and send it through your chosen messaging app. Automatic sending requires a configured provider and your opt-in. Personal invitations are emailed after you choose the recipient and send through the System Mix. Our production hosting and email providers process the data needed to run those services. **[Insert the verified production provider/location list.]** Optional calendar, intake, push or sign-in connections involve their respective providers. Providers and recipients can retain their own copies after a connection is disabled.

**Voice and browser storage.** Dictation uses your browser's speech-recognition feature after you choose to start it. Your browser may send audio to its speech provider. Jump in the Mix does not create a raw-audio recording or transcript archive. You can review recognized text and choose whether to save it. Temporary capture drafts stay in the current tab for up to ten minutes and are removed when loaded into the destination form. The optional spoken Today summary uses a voice the browser identifies as local. Browser storage and cookies also support sign-in and account-scoped application preferences. The current conversion-event adapter does not itself send events to an analytics provider or introduce a tracking cookie; any future collector needs its own review and disclosure.

**Access and support.** Your account and workspace permissions control access to customer data. Staff privileges are limited by role and permission, and staff access requires MFA. Customer support access to a private view requires the supported consent/view workflow and produces an audit record. Infrastructure administrators can have separate operational access; application roles do not replace the hosting account's controls.

**Your choices.** In Settings → Data & privacy, export your account data or permanently delete your account. You can edit contacts and preferences and turn optional automatic sending or notifications off. Invitation recipients can use the verified withdrawal/opt-out flow. Contact the support address above about information you cannot manage yourself. We need to verify a request before sharing private records or changing an account.

**Retention and deletion.** Saved customer information remains while needed for the account unless you delete it. Automated cleanup removes expired temporary records and ages out operational details. Resolved or closed support conversations become eligible for deletion after 180 inactive days, with exceptions for recent activity or unresolved delivery. Some operational/security details remain for up to 400 days under their activity-based rules. Limited suppression, invitation quota and replay-prevention records can remain longer so cleanup does not send unwanted mail or revive used access. Active invitations and unresolved support work have separate retention exceptions. **[Insert verified backup/provider/log retention and request handling.]** Deleting an account does not retract delivered emails, erase files you downloaded, or immediately remove data from historical provider backups.

**Changes and questions.** We will update the effective date when this notice changes. **[Confirm the operator's notification process for material changes.]** Use **[support email]** for questions and requests.
