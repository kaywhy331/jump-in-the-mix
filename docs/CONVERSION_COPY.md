# Conversion copy decisions

Updated September 10, 2026. These are message hypotheses supported by the implemented product, not claims of measured conversion improvement.

## September 10 implementation

The shared homepage/profession experience now follows the five-chapter blueprint. The homepage headline is “Good conversations deserve a next step,” paired with a literal category and subhead. The demo uses fixed fictional contacts and never sends. Access starts with one person and a reason to reconnect; the sending FAQ explains default app handoffs, optional configured-provider sending, and reviewed System Mix invitation email sent by Jump in the Mix.

Both supplied Joanna Wiebe references are indexed in [assets/README.md](../assets/README.md). The [current copy/claim matrix](HOMEPAGE_COPY_AND_CLAIM_MATRIX_2026-09-10.md) records source reconciliation and editorial fixes. The [headline review](HOMEPAGE_HEADLINE_REVIEW_2026-09-10.md) retains the previous direct headline as a comparison control and supplies 25 candidates. No replacement headline or measured winner has been selected. Participant research remains pending.

## September 8 historical decisions

The following records the earlier implementation and rationale; use the September 10 matrix for current copy.

Joanna Wiebe’s [headline guidance](https://copyhackers.com/2013/09/writing-powerful-headlines/) emphasizes matching visitor expectations, clear language, getting to the reader’s concern, and communicating a useful benefit. Her [message-hierarchy example](https://copyhackers.com/2016/06/copywriting-principles-sweatblock/) gives each message a purpose rather than treating a page as an assortment of sections.

The homepage now leads with “Know who to follow up with. And what to say.” The subhead explains the actual sequence: keep contacts together, choose a time, personalize a prepared message, use the daily list. Business, personal, and network uses remain visible. The interactive demo and explicitly fictional daily list provide observable product evidence.

The primary next step is joining the free-account waitlist. Nearby copy explains email confirmation and access by invitation. The FAQ answers cost, contact import, sending control, data control, and invitation selection. It now accurately distinguishes review-first handoffs, optional connected-provider sending, and direct System Mix invitation email. The closing copy describes the first useful task and what happens after signup. Since September 11 the FAQ lives on its own `/faq` page, linked from the homepage header and every public footer, rather than closing the homepage.

No customer testimonials, usage statistics, revenue claims, conversion lifts, invented deadlines, guaranteed selection dates, or guarantees of viral growth were added. Weekly selection explains the real rule: up to ten eligible requests every seven days, five oldest and five random. A signup is not an account or an invitation.

Still required before claiming validated marketing effectiveness: collect actual prospect/customer language, observe new-user comprehension and task completion, identify the highest-intent audience, and measure confirmed waitlist requests → issued invitations → verified accounts → completed useful follow-ups. Keep private contact content and access URLs out of those measurements. Use those observations to revise the message rather than inventing customer research.
