## Uncoached tasks

1. Look at the page and explain what you believe the product does.
2. Complete the fictional example in the sandbox.
3. Explain what the sandbox did and did not do.
4. Explain how you expect access to work after requesting it.
5. Describe one recent situation where this kind of follow-up would or would not fit.

## Headline comparison for the first two groups

Use the [headline comparison brief](HOMEPAGE_HEADLINE_REVIEW_2026-09-10.md) for H01/H02/H23 cards and rotation. Record current headline ID, alternate-card order, prior exposure, and per-card comprehension before preference. The consulting route begins with H16; keep that first impression separate from homepage H01 observations. Editorial scores are not participant results.

Show the alternate headline only after the first unprompted task. Keep the category label, subhead and surrounding layout fixed. Ask what each version means, what they expect after clicking and which version sets the more accurate expectation. Record comprehension evidence before preference. The product owner records the selected wording and reason in the copy/claim matrix.

## Controlled first-use check

Use a test account and controlled recipient. Ask the participant to start with one person, choose a contact method, add private context, choose timing, review the draft, and record Sent or Not yet. Record coaching, duplicate objects, unexpected campaign enrollment, lost context and recovery from back/retry/skip. Never infer provider delivery or recipient response from a user-recorded Sent outcome.
