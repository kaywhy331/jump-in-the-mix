# Homepage experience research protocol

Use this protocol for the human validation gates in P1, P4 and P6. It is prepared for execution; no participant results are claimed. Record each session and cohort in the [research session and cohort worksheet](HOMEPAGE_RESEARCH_SESSION_TEMPLATE_2026-09-10.md).

Recruit established independent professionals who can describe a recent unfinished follow-up and choose their own tools. Start with five real-estate agents and five consultants, then five photographers, five owner-operated painting contractors and five independent recruiters. Treat profession, age and biography as secondary to the behavioral fit. Obtain consent before recording; store anonymized observations outside product analytics.

Give each participant the matching direct route without explaining the product. Ask them to say what they believe it does, complete the fictional example, explain what happened, describe the access sequence, and name one appropriate real situation. Record time, coaching, mistaken-send beliefs, uncertainty, objections, current workaround and whether the example felt relevant. Then compare the still version with any motion variant while copy and interaction stay fixed.

Pass an initial group when at least four of five participants independently identify follow-up software, complete within two minutes, understand that nothing sent, describe confirmation followed by invitation, and name an appropriate use. Any belief that the sandbox sent or scheduled a real action requires revision and retest. Report counts and raw uncertainty; this small sample does not establish conversion lift or market demand.

Do not ask participants to enter real client information into the public sandbox. Test actual handoffs only in invited test accounts with controlled recipients and let users record “Not yet.” Recruitment, consent and live contact are external activities requiring the product owner’s coordination.
